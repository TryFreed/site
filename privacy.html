Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

TryFreed ("we", "our", or "us") is operated by João Vitor Gomes Torres, an individual based in Massachusetts, USA. This policy covers the TryFreed website, the TryFreed web app, and the TryFreed mobile apps for iPhone and Android. It explains what information we collect, how we use it, who helps us process it, and your rights regarding your data.

Sections 9 (Fredy, our AI assistant) and 10 (your rights) are also available in Portuguese below. / As seções 9 (Fredy, nosso assistente de IA) e 10 (seus direitos) também estão em português abaixo.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

  • Email address (used for login and account recovery)
  • Password (stored as a secure hash — we never see your plain-text password)
  • Profile details you choose to add: your name and, if you use the couple features, your partner's name and marital status
  • Subscription status and a Stripe customer ID, so we know your subscription is active

Financial data you enter

  • Income and expense records you create inside the app
  • Savings goals, goal progress, and debt tracking entries
  • Budget categories and notes

Technical data

  • When you connect to our website or servers, our providers process technical data such as your IP address, browser or device type, and request times, to deliver the service and keep it secure.

We do not collect bank credentials, credit card numbers, Social Security numbers, or any data from financial institutions. TryFreed does not connect to your bank and does not move money. All financial data in TryFreed is entered by you. We do not use advertising or analytics tools, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate TryFreed on the web and on your phone
  • To sync your data across your devices
  • To process your subscription payment via Stripe
  • To send you transactional emails (e.g., password reset, billing receipts)
  • To answer your questions to Fredy, only after you agree (see Section 9)
  • To find and fix errors and keep the service secure, using the limited error reports and technical logs described in Section 5

We never sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

We use a limited set of third-party services to operate TryFreed:

  • Supabase — Cloud database, authentication, and server functions. Your account and financial data are stored in Supabase's infrastructure. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Stripe — Payment processing. Stripe handles all billing and card data; we never store your card number. Your email and billing history are kept in our Stripe account. Stripe Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic — AI processing (Claude API) for Fredy, used only after you tap "Allow and continue" on the consent screen. See Section 9. Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Pages — Hosts the files of our website. GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Cloudflare — Sits in front of our website as content delivery network (CDN) and DNS, and helps protect it from attacks. It processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver pages. Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • ntfy.sh — Delivers short internal alerts to the operator's phone (for example, "a Fredy response was reported" or "an error happened in the web app"). Alerts are designed not to include your name, email, amounts, or message content.
  • Apple and Google — Distribute our mobile apps through the App Store and Google Play, under their own privacy policies.

Each provider processes data under its own terms and privacy policy. We share with each one only what it needs to do its job.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in Supabase, which uses industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit (TLS 1.2+). We apply row-level security so that only your authenticated account can access your records. On your phone, your login session is stored encrypted.

5. Mobile App, Device Features, and Diagnostics

  • Camera and photos. The app uses your camera, or opens your photos, only when you tap to add a receipt or screenshot for Fredy. On Android we use the system photo picker, so the app never gets access to your whole photo library. Photos are sent to Fredy only after you agree to the consent screen (Section 9).
  • Face ID and fingerprint. If you turn on the app lock, your phone checks your face or fingerprint. TryFreed only receives a yes or no. Your biometric data never leaves your device, and we never receive it.
  • Reminders. If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled on your phone and don't go through our servers. A reminder may show a bill's name and due date (for example, "Rent is due tomorrow") on your lock screen. You can turn reminders off in the app's Settings or in your phone's settings.
  • Voice on the website. On the website, the microphone button uses your browser's built-in speech recognition. Depending on your browser, the browser maker may process the audio under its own privacy policy. TryFreed never receives your audio; only the resulting text goes into the message box. The mobile app does not use the microphone.
  • Stored on your device. Your encrypted login session, app preferences, the date you agreed to the AI consent screen, and a backup copy of any AI response report that couldn't be sent are kept on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes them, and deleting your account in the app clears them too.
  • Export. You can export your data to an Excel file at any time: Settings → Export in the mobile app, or Export Excel on the website. The file is created on your device, and you decide where to save or share it.
  • Error reports (website only). If the web app hits a technical error, it may send a short error message (up to 160 characters) and where in the app it happened. Before it's sent, numbers, email addresses, and links are removed. It arrives as an alert on the operator's phone and is not stored in our database. The mobile app does not send error reports.
  • Technical logs. Our server provider keeps short-term technical logs of requests (for example, time, status, and, in some error cases, your account ID) for security and troubleshooting. These logs do not contain your messages, photos, or financial entries.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. If you cancel your subscription, your data stays available until you delete your account.

When you delete your account (in the app, on the website, or by email; see tryfreed.com/delete-account), your account, profile, income, bills, goals, goal history, debts, AI usage counters, and AI response reports are deleted from our database right away. Backup copies are deleted within 30 days. Deleting your account also cancels any active subscription immediately.

What we keep after deletion:

  • Your email and billing history in our Stripe account, for as long as tax and accounting laws require.
  • Emails you sent to our support address.
  • Short-term technical logs described in Section 5.
  • Content you sent to Fredy, which Anthropic deletes within 30 days, unless it must keep it longer to enforce its usage policy or to comply with the law (see Section 9).

7. Age Requirement

TryFreed is intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by email or via an in-app notice. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

9. Fredy, Our AI Assistant / Fredy, Nosso Assistente de IA

EN — What happens when you use Fredy

Fredy is optional. It runs on the Claude API from Anthropic. Before your first message, on the website and in the mobile app, we show a consent screen. Nothing is sent to Anthropic before you tap "Allow and continue." If you tap "Not now", nothing is sent.

When you use Fredy, we send the following to Anthropic, through our server:

  • what you type (or dictate on the website) to Fredy;
  • receipt photos or screenshots you choose to send, and the text of spreadsheets you choose to share (spreadsheets are read on your device; only their text is sent);
  • a summary of your plan, so the answer makes sense: the names, amounts, and dates of your income, bills, goals, and debts, and the numbers TryFreed calculates (such as how much to set aside). Entries are marked only as "you" or "your partner"; your name and your partner's name are not sent.

All data travels over HTTPS (TLS). TryFreed does not store your photos on its servers, and your conversation with Fredy is kept only on your device while you use it, not on TryFreed's servers. Anthropic, however, keeps what we send and Fredy's replies for up to 30 days and then deletes them automatically, unless it must keep them longer to enforce its usage policy or to comply with the law. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not use this content to train its models. See Anthropic's privacy policy.

Turning Fredy off: You can withdraw your consent at any time in Settings. After that, nothing more is sent to Anthropic unless you agree again.

Reporting a response: In the mobile app, each Fredy reply has a "Report response" option. A report stores the reason you choose, the text of that reply (up to 1,000 characters), your language, your platform, and your account ID, so we can review it and improve Fredy. Other users can't see reports. The operator gets a phone alert with only the reason and platform, not the content. Reports are deleted when you delete your account.

Fredy can make mistakes. Check every amount before you confirm. Fredy does not give professional financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.

PT — O que acontece quando você usa o Fredy

O Fredy é opcional e funciona com a API Claude, da Anthropic. Antes da primeira mensagem, no site e no app de celular, mostramos uma tela de consentimento. Nada é enviado à Anthropic antes de você tocar em "Permitir e continuar". Se você tocar em "Agora não", nada é enviado.

Quando você usa o Fredy, enviamos à Anthropic, pelo nosso servidor:

  • o que você escreve (ou dita, no site) para o Fredy;
  • fotos de recibos ou prints que você escolhe mandar, e o texto das planilhas que você escolhe compartilhar (a planilha é lida no seu aparelho; só o texto é enviado);
  • um resumo do seu plano, para a resposta fazer sentido: nomes, valores e datas das suas rendas, contas, metas e dívidas, e os números que o TryFreed calcula (como quanto separar). Os lançamentos são marcados só como "você" ou "seu cônjuge"; seu nome e o do seu cônjuge não são enviados.

Tudo trafega por HTTPS (TLS). O TryFreed não guarda suas fotos nos servidores dele, e a conversa com o Fredy fica só no seu aparelho enquanto você usa, não nos servidores do TryFreed. A Anthropic, porém, guarda o que enviamos e as respostas do Fredy por até 30 dias e depois apaga automaticamente, a menos que precise guardar por mais tempo para aplicar a política de uso dela ou cumprir a lei. Pelos termos comerciais dela, a Anthropic não usa esse conteúdo para treinar modelos. Veja a política de privacidade da Anthropic.

Desligar o Fredy: Você pode retirar o consentimento quando quiser em Configurações. A partir daí nada mais vai para a Anthropic, a menos que você aceite de novo.

Reportar uma resposta: No app de celular, cada resposta do Fredy tem a opção "Reportar resposta". O reporte guarda o motivo escolhido, o texto daquela resposta (até 1.000 caracteres), o idioma, a plataforma e o ID da sua conta, para analisarmos e melhorarmos o Fredy. Outros usuários não veem os reportes. O operador recebe no celular um aviso só com o motivo e a plataforma, sem o conteúdo. Os reportes são apagados quando você exclui a conta.

O Fredy pode errar. Confira cada valor antes de confirmar. O Fredy não dá conselho financeiro, de investimento, fiscal ou jurídico profissional.

10. Your Rights / Seus Direitos

EN — Your data rights (all users)

  • Access: You can see all your data in TryFreed at any time, and you can ask us for a copy.
  • Portability: Export your data to Excel at any time: Settings → Export in the mobile app, or Export Excel on the website.
  • Correction: Edit or delete any entry directly in the app at any time.
  • Deletion: Delete your account in the mobile app (Settings → Delete my account), on the website (menu → Delete my account), or by email, following the steps at tryfreed.com/delete-account. See Section 6 for what is deleted and what we keep.

To make a request, email support@tryfreed.com from the email address linked to your account, with the subject "Data Rights Request". We verify requests through that email address. We will never ask for your password. We respond within 30 days.

Para fazer um pedido, mande um email para support@tryfreed.com a partir do email da sua conta, com o assunto "Pedido de Direitos sobre Dados". Confirmamos o pedido por esse email. Nunca pedimos sua senha. Respondemos em até 30 dias.
